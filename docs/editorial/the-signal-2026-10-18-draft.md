## Small tech wins for a calmer week
Week of October 18, 2026 — EDITORIAL DRAFT / NOT READY TO PUBLISH

### Reader copy begins
Technology should make everyday life a little easier—not turn your weekend into a troubleshooting session. This week's draft brings together three approachable ideas from our October 13–17 content briefs. Choose one small project, then leave time for the people you love.

#### An easier way into your Google Account
If another password reset feels familiar, explore Google's passkeys. They let you sign in using a fingerprint, face unlock or your device's screen-lock PIN. Your biometric information stays on your device. Start on a device you personally own, not a shared computer, and keep account recovery information current. This suits anyone who wants to understand a simpler sign-in option before changing their routine.
Official guide: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/13548313?hl=en

#### Make something with the grandkids
Scratch offers a friendly introduction to creative coding. Instead of typing complex commands, you connect colorful blocks. Try a tiny Halloween challenge together: have a character say “Boo,” then move. Ask the child to predict what changing the number of steps will do. The point is shared curiosity, not getting everything right the first time.
Official beginner guide: https://scratchfoundation.org/learn/learning-library/getting-started

#### Give an indoor lamp a bedtime
The Tapo P125M is a smart plug with scheduled on/off control. For someone starting a small smart-home project, a compatible indoor lamp is a manageable place to begin. Set separate ON and OFF actions, test both, and follow the device's electrical and setup requirements. This is a product idea, not a hands-on review or a verified discount.
Official product information: https://www.tapo.com/us/product/tapo-p125m-smart-plug/v1/1-pack/

#### One news update worth knowing
On October 15, Google announced changes to AI Mode and AI Overviews designed to make web sources easier to find. Some features are rolling out or being tested, so everyone's screen may not look the same. Our practical takeaway: open the original source before relying on an AI summary or buying something based on it.
Announcement: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/updates-ai-mode-overviews-explore-web/

#### A thoughtful start to gift season
Halloween is approaching, but there is no need to rush a tech purchase. First check what someone already uses, what would genuinely help, and whether the new device will work with it. Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days ran October 6–7 and has ended; this edition makes no current sale claims.
Official event record: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-big-deals-day-2026-when-october-6-7

Finish with one small win—and a little time away from the screen.
